What's going on here

Tap the green button to walk the crew through the run. Each step captures its record once, and that record flows to QA, the client portal, stock and the invoice.

"Scan liner" uses the phone camera on the reel's QR label. The system checks the reel is allocated to this job, in date and the right DN before the crew can start.

Design rules: 3 taps or fewer per step, 48px+ targets for gloves, readable in sunlight, voice notes allowed, nothing lost without signal.
